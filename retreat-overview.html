The biennial AIBP Innovation Retreat brings together a select group of ASEAN stakeholders in a single location.

Activities are curated to allow participants to access an intra-ASEAN network to assess the level of Innovation and Growth they are pursuing in their own organisations, allowing them to bring back fresh ideas and advocate for change within their own organisations.

This year, we welcomed

71 PARTICIPANTS from 5 COUNTRIES across SOUTHEAST ASIA
